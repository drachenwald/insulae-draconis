These are the local groups around the United Kingdom, Ireland, and Iceland. You are welcome to get in touch with anyone listed – the Newcomer welcome officer (Chatelaine/Castellan) or local group President (Seneschal) are a good place to start.

Many of the local practices and social gatherings are shown in our [Upcoming Events calendar](https://insulaedraconis.org/events/#/), and all are welcome. They are usually free or a nominal donation to attend, and can be a great place to meet people before you join. 

If you're looking for a group elsewhere in Europe or in South Africa, visit the [Drachenwald Website](https://drachenwald.sca.org/).
