Local practices and social evenings are informal, everyday-clothes affairs — usually free to come and watch, with perhaps a small donation towards the room. Scheduled events run from a single day to a full week of camping, with historic-style clothing and a site fee for everyone attending: from a few pounds or euros for a day, to around £/€100 for a full weekend with meals and a bed, with camping events varying by size and length.

Learn more about what to expect at events.

If you are planning an event in Insulae Draconis, submit it to us with our google form.
All our events are subject to the Drachenwald Code of Conduct.
