--- title: Principality of Insulae Draconis subtitle: Learn history by living it ---

Ever wanted to try archery, learn to sword fight, sew your own historical clothing, or sit down to a candlelit feast? Insulae Draconis is a community of people across England, Ireland, Scotland, Wales and Iceland who meet up most weeks to make things, learn skills, compete, and enjoy each other's company, inspired by the arts and life of the Middle Ages and beyond. Whether you're drawn to combat, crafts, cooking, music or just the company, visitors are always welcome to come along and watch, no experience necessary. We're part of the worldwide Society for Creative Anachronism (SCA).
